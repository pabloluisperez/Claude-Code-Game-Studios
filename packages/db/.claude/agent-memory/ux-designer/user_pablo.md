Pablo is the project owner and creative director for Cascada FC. He communicates in Spanish and expects responses in Spanish. He is comfortable with UX theory references (Norman, Fitts's Law, affordances, progressive disclosure, mental models).

He explicitly requests adversarial reviews — finding problems, not validating what already exists. He provides well-structured briefs that include both documented concepts and undocumented prototype learnings, and expects the UX designer to treat both as evidence.

He separates concerns clearly: the document describes outcomes, the prototype learnings describe discovered problems. He expects the UX reviewer to surface the gap between the two.
