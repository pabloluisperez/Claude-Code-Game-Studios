# Stadium Progression — Nano Banana Prompts

10-level visual progression of the stadium for the game's "Stadium" screen
header image. Generated with **Nano Banana** (Gemini 2.5 Flash Image).

## Scope Note

City management has been removed from gameplay scope. The rich urban context
in these prompts is **purely atmospheric** — visual storytelling of the
stadium's growth over time. There is no city management feature behind it.

## Series Consistency Rules

For the 10 images to feel like the same place evolving (not 10 different
stadiums), three blocks must stay **identical** across all prompts:

- `LIGHTING` (golden hour, sun from upper-left)
- `STYLE` (painterly, Two Point + Ghibli reference)
- `COMPOSITION` (16:9 cinematic, 3/4 aerial 35°)

Plus a fixed set of **continuity anchors** that must appear in every image
(in increasingly preserved/historic form as the city modernizes):

- The parish church steeple one block away
- A green public bench
- A red postbox
- Geranium planters in window boxes
- Ivy climbing old walls
- A stray cat on a low wall
- A fragment of the original cobblestone alley
- Cypress-clad hills on the distant horizon
- The river bend at the back

If Nano Banana produces a frame where the sun has moved, the angle has
shifted, or the church/anchors have disappeared, **regenerate** — do not
accept that frame into the series.

**Generation order**: produce **Level 1 and Level 10 first**. They are the
extremes. If the style is coherent between those two, the intermediate
levels will slot in naturally.

## Level Roadmap

| Level | Stadium | City | Visual milestone |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dirt pitch, wooden poles tied with rope | Old town, 80s cars, sparse | Zero infrastructure |
| 2 | Grass, wooden bleachers one side | 90s cars, streetlights, lively | First infrastructure |
| 3 | Grass, concrete stands two sides | 70s mid-rise mixed in | Ticket booth + scoreboard |
| 4 | Full bowl four sides, real floodlights | Avenue, parking, shops | Changing rooms + access |
| 5 | Enclosed bowl, covered main stand | Bus terminal, first tower | Plastic seats |
| 6 | Two-tier bowl + full roof | Urban center, metro, offices | Integrated LED perimeter |
| 7 | Three tiers + VIP boxes + ceramic facade | Young metropolis, tower cranes | **Architectural facade** |
| 8 | Partial retractable roof + diamond LED facade | Real skyline, tram, skywalk | Illuminated diamond panels |
| 9 | Retractable roof + iconic arches + 4 tiers | Entertainment district, mag-lev hint | Curved metallic scales |
| 10 | Dynamic LED skin + drones + holograms + 5 tiers | Future metropolis, mag-lev, air taxis | **Climax** |

## Level 1 — Bare neighborhood dirt pitch

```
A bare neighborhood dirt football pitch wedged into a small old town, viewed
from an elevated 3/4 aerial perspective looking down at roughly a 35-degree
angle from the upper-left. Cinematic, static beauty shot for a game's "Stadium"
screen header. This is the humblest, earliest version of a future football
ground.

THE PITCH (center of frame, ~55% of composition):
A rectangular patch of bare brown dirt and dust — uneven, hard-packed earth
with scattered tufts of dry yellow grass, small stones, and a faint footworn
track around the perimeter. White chalk lines drawn directly on the dirt,
crooked and partly rubbed away. Two crude goalposts at each end made of rough
unpainted wooden poles lashed together with rope — no proper net, just a sagging
loop of frayed twine across the top, one post slightly leaning.

NO bleachers, NO seating, NO fencing of any kind around the pitch — just the
raw dirt rectangle meeting the cobblestones at its edges. A single hand-painted
wooden sign nailed to a nearby wall reads only as a small rectangle (no
legible text). A weathered ball sits forgotten near one goal.

THE URBAN CONTEXT (wrapping the pitch):
The pitch is wedged into a real city block — narrow cobblestone streets run
along all four sides, lined with three- and four-story old townhouses with
terracotta roofs, wrought-iron balconies, peeling shutters in pastel colors
(ochre, dusty pink, faded blue, cream).

A modest row of parked cars along the near curb — fewer than the future
upgrade, just two or three older 1980s European vehicles, a beat-up van, a
bicycle leaning against a streetlight. Cast-iron streetlights with frosted
glass globes at regular intervals. A zebra crossing painted in faded white.

Small details: a green public bench, a postbox, planters with geraniums, ivy
climbing one wall, laundry strung between two balconies, a tiny corner bar
with a striped awning, a parish church steeple rising one block away, a
stray cat on a low wall.

In the further distance: red-tiled rooftops descending toward distant hills,
a river bend glinting, cypress trees, the soft haze of a Mediterranean town.

LIGHTING: warm golden hour, sun low from the upper-left, long soft shadows of
streetlights and balconies stretching across cobblestones. Sky is a gradient
from peach near the horizon to clear blue above, with a few wispy cirrus clouds.

STYLE: stylized semi-realistic painterly illustration, soft hand-painted
brushwork, rich detail but clean readable shapes, gentle ambient occlusion,
warm earthy palette (terracotta, ochre, olive, dusty pastels, golden light).
Inspired by the cozy detailed look of "Two Point Campus" meets a Studio Ghibli
European townscape. Painterly, not photo-realistic.

COMPOSITION: wide cinematic 16:9 framing, pitch centered slightly below the
horizon line, urban detail filling the foreground and sides, distant skyline
on the upper third. High level of detail throughout — this is a static hero
image, not a thumbnail.

NEGATIVE: no people, no readable text, no team logos, no sponsor advertising,
no modern LED billboards, no plastic stadium seats, no skyscrapers, no neon,
no UI elements or overlays, no watermarks, no bleachers, no fences around the
pitch, no proper goal nets.
```
