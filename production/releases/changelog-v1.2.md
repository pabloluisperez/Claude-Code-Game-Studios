the "voice of the world" (press, mayors, staff). Pablo decided 2026-05-25 to
**drop the LLM entirely** in favour of a sophisticated template generator

fast, 100% Spanish — was overwhelmingly favourable.

## ✨ What ships in v1.2

### 1. Scouting market — fully functional offer flow

You can now **make offers** on any player from the `/scouting` page:

- 🟢 Click "Ofertar" on a player → modal opens with fee + sueldo + duración inputs
- Backend evaluates via F2 (free agents, wage-only) or F3 (AI clubs, fee + wage with bargain factor + need factor)
- Three deterministic outcomes:
  - **✅ Oferta aceptada** — fee + wage finalised, transfer recorded
  - **🤝 Counter offer** — AI club proposes a higher fee; you can submit a new offer
  - **❌ Rechazo** — either wage too low (free agent) or fee far below market value (AI club hard reject)
- Free agents skip the fee field automatically (only sueldo matters)
- Persists in `transfer_offers` with resolved status

### 2. Player schema: contract lifecycle

`players` table now tracks three states (`contract_status`):
- `in_contract` (default) — under active contract
- `expiring` — within 8 weeks of contract end
- `free_agent` — no current club; bid wage-only

Plus `weeks_unsigned` counter (F2 desperation discount kicks in past 20 weeks)
and `wage_expectation_eur_k_week` per player.

### 3. 📰 Press articles — narrative engine in action

After each match-day, if your club played a **notable** fixture (goal diff ≥ 3,
or 0-0), a press article appears in `/inbox` with the lead "📰 Crónica de prensa".
Generated by the narrative template engine with:

- 5 template groups filtered by goal-diff bucket
- Each group has 3 variants
- Each variant has 3-5 vocab slots × 10-15 word vocabularies
- Result: ~thousands of distinct outputs across the first 20 weeks, deterministic per (week, matchday)

Example outputs (same week, different goalDiff):
- `Vimos a un FC Cascada glorioso: 5-0. la afición estalla.`
- `Empate sin gloria ni pena: FC Cascada y Real Pueblo firmaron 0-0. el vestuario respira hondo.`
- `Derrota agónica: el FC Cascada cayó contra el Madrid, 0-2. la afición rumia.`

### 4. 🏛 Llamada del alcalde — career milestone

When you hit the **"10 partidos"** milestone, the alcalde of your club's city
calls you in `/inbox` with a template-generated message that mentions your city
name and offers political support for stadium upgrades.

### 5. 💼 Finance director weekly commentary

When the club enters "En Riesgo" or "Crisis" tier, the finance director
emits a varied weekly commentary in `/inbox` urging action. When the club
holds a positive balance over 1000 k€, congratulatory messages appear instead.

### 6. Narrative template engine (new infrastructure)

The new `packages/shared/src/sim/narrative/` module provides:

- **Engine** with deterministic seeded picks (Mulberry32-ish hash mixing)
- **Slot types**:
  - `{variable}` — context substitution
  - `{vocab:category}` — pick from a vocab table
  - `{?var?segment?}` — conditional inclusion
- **Vocab** — 10 categories of Spanish words (~150 unique terms)
- **Library** — 5 template-group collections covering match outcomes, financial milestones, derby press, mayor calls

Available for any consumer module to use. Pure functions, fully testable, deterministic per seed.

### 7. UX polish + bug fixes

- **`/squad`**: full first name in roster row (was showing only initial "L. Morán")
- **Sidebar**: "🔍 Scouting" entry added (was missing in v1.1 closeout)
- **`/finance`**: "Reformas del estadio" row in cashflow breakdown
- **`/stadium`**: "Balance del club" synced with `financial_balance` (was reading `clubs.budget`, inconsistent with /dashboard)
- **Topbar**: shows "Pretemporada" until `season.startWeek`, then "Jornada N"
- **/city tab fix**: "En Riesgo" panel links to `/finance?tab=…` now switch the tab correctly
- **Stadium installments**: reforms charged week-by-week (was upfront full debit); player sees the installment in the cashflow row

## 🐛 Known issues / surface-able in playtest

- Free agents don't yet exist in the seed data — the offer flow works but you need to scout an "expiring" player or wait for the AI rotation worker (v1.3) to spawn them. Until then the F2 branch is technically reachable but rarely triggered.
- The narrative engine's vocabulary is Spanish-only — no i18n. v1.3+ could add `en` and `pt-BR` vocab tables.
- Press articles fire only on goal diff ≥ 3 or 0-0. A "narrow win" (e.g., 1-0) doesn't trigger press coverage. Designed this way to avoid spam.
