## Director Panel Assessment (lean mode — all 4 spawned)

### Creative Director: **READY**

The creative vision survived the slice playtest with high fidelity. The four
pillars are intact and demonstrably expressed: cascade-engine GDD +
match-simulation deliver "Tinkering > Optimization"; economy + league-system
give "World Is The Scoreboard" tangible weight; manager-rpg directly embodies
"You Grow Like Your Club"; and the DOM-only MVP scope plus deferred AI
narrative protects "Calm Is The Tempo." The discover-cascades fantasy clearly
landed — the 6 production OQs are refinement signals about *surfacing*
cascades better, not "the cascade fantasy doesn't work" pivots, which is
exactly the feedback profile you want at this gate. Creatively, advance. The
remaining work is execution scaffolding, not vision validation.

### Technical Director: **CONCERNS**

The 12 Accepted ADRs are internally coherent and the vertical slice has
empirically validated the riskiest pattern (ADR-013 Option B pause/resume
determinism via 34/34 passing tests), which is the strongest possible evidence
that the simulation core is implementable. However, three foundational
artifacts are missing that Production sprint planning depends on:

1. `architecture.md` as the master synthesis document — without it,
   specialists will re-derive the system topology per sprint and drift
2. `control-manifest.md` — programmers need an enforceable rules sheet
   distilled from ADRs (forbidden patterns, layer boundaries, PRNG discipline,
   server-authoritative state) before parallel feature work begins
3. Requirements traceability matrix — needed to verify that the 2-pillar MVP
   scope maps cleanly to ADR coverage with no orphaned requirements

The Foundation-layer circular dependency check is also unrun — cheap to
execute, expensive to discover mid-sprint. Recommend: do NOT advance stage.txt
until these three documents land (estimate 1-2 days of TD work). The ADRs
themselves are production-ready; the connective tissue is not.

### Producer: **CONCERNS** (NOT READY today, READY in 2-3 days)

### Art Director: **CONCERNS**

Art bible is structurally complete (all 9 sections, approved 2026-05-16) and
AD-ART-BIBLE sign-off was consciously deferred under lean mode — acceptable.
Three concerns before Production:

1. **Art bible was written for v1.1+ canvas, not MVP DOM-only**. Sections 1-6
   assume a live isometric PixiJS canvas world. The MVP has no canvas. OQ-HUD-11
   introduces pixel-art sprites delivered as `<img>` inside DOM — a hybrid the
   art bible does not formally address. Without an explicit DOM-boundary
   section or addendum, the bible misleads MVP asset production.
2. **No entity inventory or asset spec catalog**. `design/assets/entity-inventory.md`
   does not exist. For Production, Pablo needs to know precisely which assets
   ship in MVP versus v1.1. The OQ-HUD-11 pixel-art sprites (goal animation,
   stretcher, sliding tackle) are the only confirmed MVP pixel assets — but
   they are undocumented in any asset spec. Production risk: scope creep into
   "just one more sprite."
3. **MVP visual identity is under-defined relative to what will actually ship**.
   The DOM management UI receives no visual spec equivalent in weight to the
   isometric sections. Section 7 (UI/HUD) exists but was written with the
   canvas present as context. MVP players see only the DOM for 4-6 months.

**Recommended resolution**: (a) add a one-page "MVP Scope Boundary" addendum
to art bible; (b) create `design/assets/entity-inventory.md` as a minimal list
(DOM components + 3 OQ-HUD-11 sprite types). 30-minute session each.
