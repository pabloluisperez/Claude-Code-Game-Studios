### Producer — 🔴 NOT READY

**Dominant concern**: scope.

- 4-pillar MVP (soccer manager + city builder + manager-RPG + AI narrative) for solo dev in 4-6 months is 4 games stacked
- Reference points: Football Manager (50+ devs, 1 year/release); Cities Skylines (3 years small team); Wildermyth (6 years)
- ~80 productive days ÷ 4 pillars = 20 days per pillar — not viable

**Strong recommendation**: cut MVP to 2 pillars. Suggested: Soccer manager (core) + Manager-RPG (progression hook). Defer city builder + AI narrative to v1.1 / v2.0.

Other findings:
- No system GDDs = no acceptance criteria = no sprintable work (#1 cause of solo-dev project death)
- No vertical slice = unvalidated assumption that cascade-engine + match-sim + narrative-AI compose
- Stage/artifact mismatch is informative, **do NOT advance stage.txt**

Proposed minimum viable path: 3 months pre-prod work (scope summit → 2 GDDs → art bible Sec 1-4 → vertical slice → gate-check re-run → advance stage).
